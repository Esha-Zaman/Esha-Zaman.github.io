Computational research

Structure-Based Drug Repurposing in Leishmania tropica

A computational workflow to investigate drug-resistance-associated protein targets and prioritize repurposed compounds.

2025–2026Manuscript being prepared
Conceptual illustration · not experimental or manuscript data
01 / Research objective

What was the question?

Investigate whether selected repurposed compounds could be computationally prioritized against resistance-associated proteins of Leishmania tropica.

02 / My role

What I contributed

  • Investigated and characterized protein targets starting from sequence data.
  • Modelled, refined and assessed selected protein structures.
  • Prepared candidate ligands and interpreted docking and ADMET predictions.
03 / Interpretation

Outcome & learning

A ranked computational assessment of candidates was developed to guide future research; predicted activity requires experimental validation.

Methods & tools
  • Whole-genome and protein annotation
  • Homology modelling and structure validation
  • Molecular docking and interaction analysis
  • Drug-likeness and ADMET screening
Sharing status

Public summary only. Original docking images, binding scores, unpublished figures, and any future molecular-dynamics outputs are intentionally withheld pending author/supervisor clearance and publication.
