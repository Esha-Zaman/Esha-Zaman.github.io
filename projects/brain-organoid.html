Research proposal

Nanomaterial Investigation in Brain Organoids for Alzheimer’s Disease

A collaborative research synopsis combining organoid models, nanomaterials, and possible molecular readouts for Alzheimer’s disease.

2024Proposal / synopsis
Conceptual illustration · not experimental or manuscript data
01 / Research objective

What was the question?

Design a proposed strategy to study a mica-derived nanomaterial (STB-MP) in brain-organoid models of Alzheimer’s disease.

02 / My role

What I contributed

  • Participated as a named contributor on the group synopsis.
  • Contributed to the formulation of a research design linking disease modelling and nanomaterial evaluation.
03 / Interpretation

Outcome & learning

Produced a prospective research plan. No organoid experiments or therapeutic efficacy results are claimed.

Methods & tools
  • Hypothesis and synopsis development
  • Organoid model study design
  • Proposed molecular assays
  • Nanomedicine literature review
Sharing status

Research proposal only; descriptions of assays are planned methods, not performed experiments.
